THE FLOW OF HOW WE KNOW

Scroll Down ↓
You know…books, the written word, and literacy was a rare thing among the masses. It was a status symbol in society the world over. Vast amounts of man-hours dedicated to copying books by hand. Information was filtered through elites in society…
The Gutenberg Press - 1440
Until The Gutenberg Press was invented. This made knowledge attainable for the masses. It usurped the Elite’s and the Church’s monopoly on information, casting aside these early gatekeepers.
It reshaped society completely. Law, religion, science. It was a Renaissance, with ideas, beliefs, knowledge, and culture no longer trapped within borders.
